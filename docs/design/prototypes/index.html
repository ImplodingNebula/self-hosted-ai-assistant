Design prototypes

One self-contained, pixel-real page per theme; the rules that generalize live in DESIGN.md, and when prose and prototype disagree, the prototype wins.

Swatches show each theme's actual token values: app background, panel, text, accent, border. Paired themes (Porcelain, Minimal) split every swatch diagonally - light upper-left, dark lower-right. Phosphor's dots are its three accent variants: green, amber, red. Aurora (next) is the default theme's reference; its panel swatch is the composer's translucent fill, since its cards are lit panes rather than a panel colour.
